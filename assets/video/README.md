# Aerial surf background

`aerial-surf.mp4` is adapted from [Drone Footage of Beach Waves by Mikhail Nilov](https://www.pexels.com/video/drone-footage-of-beach-waves-8303087/), used under the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/).

The [4K source](https://videos.pexels.com/video-files/8303087/8303087-uhd_4096_2160_25fps.mp4) was cropped and resized to 2560 × 1440. The silent H.264 loop runs for 14 seconds at 24 fps, with a one-second dissolve at the seam and metadata placed first for fast startup.

The file is about 10.4 MiB and loads only after the visitor enables the background.
